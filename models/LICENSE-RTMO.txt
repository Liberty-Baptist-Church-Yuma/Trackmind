rtmo-s-u8.onnx
==============

RTMO-s (body7, 640x640) from OpenMMLab MMPose:
  https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmpose/tree/main/projects/rtmo
  Downloaded from https://download.openmmlab.com/mmpose/v1/projects/rtmo/onnx_sdk/
  rtmo-s_8xb32-600e_body7-640x640-dac2bf74_20231211.zip (end2end.onnx)

Modified by Trackmind: the input was changed to uint8 NHWC, with a Cast and a
Transpose added in front of the original graph (pose_gpu.make_uint8_input).
The network itself is unchanged.
